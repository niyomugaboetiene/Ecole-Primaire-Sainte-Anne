<h1>Project Description</h1>

Ecole Primaire Sainte Anne is a Primary School located in Southern Province, Kamonyi District. By
the agreements with Parents, the students take lunch consisting Potatoes, Maize, Rice, Beans and
Vegetables at school. The kitchen and stock chief have to report every week the stock status and used
amount of money.
Even though kitchen and stock chief is skilled in using computer, he reports using hard copies (papers),
therefore they have a problem where information can easily be lost and it is hard to produce weekly
reports about stock-in, stock-out and used amount of money.
They want to hire a web application developer to develop a web application that enables the chief after
successful login with account using UserName and Password he created to record the
Products(ProductId(PK), Product_Name) for most used products or a new product,information of
products can be Updated or Deleted when it is necessary, bought products or
Stock_in(Product_Id(FK),Date,Quantity,Unit_Price,Total_Price)
and used products or
stock_out(Product_Id(FK),Date,Quantity).
Finally, the application should help him to generate a report of stock status and used amount for specific
period/range (like day or week)
